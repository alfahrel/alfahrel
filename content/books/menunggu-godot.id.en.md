After Godot became widely known in Indonesia since 1969, when it was performed at the Big Theater, TIM, by the Theater Workshop, only now has the script materialized in book form. It is hoped that this play can stimulate more theater lovers to not only stage it, but also learn more thoroughly.

Because this play, which seems to have a pessimistic tone, actually contains extraordinary courage to maintain loyalty waiting for something to be hoped for.
It is hoped that the presence of this book can help us to remain loyal to something or someone or the situation we have been waiting for.

We need encouragement to be able to maintain our life force..
