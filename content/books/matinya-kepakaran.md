How important is vaccination? Is it true that the Earth is flat? Are eggs good to consume? In the information era like now, those who answer such questions are not only experts, but also conspiracy theorists, know-it-all lay people, and even misleading celebrities. Sometimes, in today's jungle of information, expert explanations are not heard, while answers from figures who have many followers are more trusted and endanger many people.
