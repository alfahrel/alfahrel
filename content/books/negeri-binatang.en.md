The animals of Manor Farm live oppressed by Mr. Jones' cruel treatment. One day the animals forgot to be fed so they rebelled and took over the farm from humans, so that all the animals could live freely and happily. But eventually the rebellion was betrayed, and the fate of the animals was even worse than before, under the dictatorship of a pig named Napoleon.
