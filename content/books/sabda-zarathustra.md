Thus Spoke Zarathustra is Nietzsche's most personal pen description. It is the history of his most individual experiences, namely the history of friendship, wishful thinking, joy and the darkest sorrow. Zarathustra was the first to see a wheel in the spinning of events in the battle between good and bad. Compared to any other thinker, Zarathustra is more honest and braver in uncovering all human depravity which is shrouded in various excuses that appear brilliant from the outside.
