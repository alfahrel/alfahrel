Joseph K, a bank employee, wakes up one day to find himself accused of a crime he did not commit, a crime that was never revealed to him. After being arrested, he was released, but had to report to court regularly—an annoying routine, as nothing was ever resolved. And as he becomes increasingly unsure of his fate, his personal life becomes increasingly unpredictable.
