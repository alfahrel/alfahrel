Gulliver's journey tells the story of the exploration of a ship surgeon named Lemuel Gulliver into four remote countries in the world. In Lilliput, Gulliver discovers a land in miniature where he is seen as a god. In Brobdingnag, on the other hand, he is just a small creature in a land of giants. On Laputa, a flying island, he meets a society of speculators. The final voyage takes him to the land of the Houyhnhnms, where virtuous horses coexist with Yahoo, uncivilized beings who bear a resemblance to humans.
