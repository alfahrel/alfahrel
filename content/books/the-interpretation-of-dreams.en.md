What are the most common dreams and why do we have them? What does it mean to dream about death? Symbolizes what dreams of swimming, failing, or flying?

First published by Sigmund Freud in 1899, The Interpretation of Dreams answers why we dream and what it means in the larger picture of our psychological lives. Exploring real and latent theories of dream content, the special language of dreams, dreams as the fulfillment of desires, the importance of childhood experiences, and more, Freud, widely considered the father of psychoanalysis, thoroughly and seriously examined the psychology of dreams. Covering dozens of case histories and detailed analyzes of actual dreams, this book presents Freud's legendary work as a tool for understanding our sleep experiences.
