Translated from French. This book includes original illustrations.

Candide tells the story of a naive young man who steadfastly believes that “everything is for the best” even when faced with injustice, suffering, and despair. Controversial and entertaining, this novel is highly relevant in our world, which is filled with—as Candide puts it—“the madness of insisting that everything is fine when it is actually terrible.”
