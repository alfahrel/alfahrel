Metamorphosis is noted as the only work by Kafka that has received the most public reception. It doesn't stop there, many world writers are even inspired by this novela. Gabriel Garcia Marquez admitted that when he was 19 years old he had memorized beyond the head the opening sentence of the famous Metamorphosis. Vladimir Nabokov said, "Whoever sees something in Metamorphosis that is more than just an insect fantasy, I consider it a successful reader."
