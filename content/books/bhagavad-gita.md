This is the only "drama of the human heart" written in a very poetic and deeply touching language. A work that is said to have existed since 500 years before Christ, beautifully encapsulating themes of divine philosophy, teachings of virtue, and universal ethical values.

Through the teachings of Krishna presented to Arjuna before the great battle of the Bharata family, known as the Bharata-yuda, readers can draw from the vast and profound content of these teachings, for our lives that are increasingly dry from the lack of spiritual waters and life wisdom.

Furthermore, as expressed by J.W. Hauer in the Hibbert Journal (1940), this is the essence of the Bhagavad Gita: "We are not asked to solve the problem of the meaning of life, but to find the actions demanded of us and to carry them out in dealing with the complexities of life."
