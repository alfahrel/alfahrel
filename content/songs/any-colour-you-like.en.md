## About
An instrumental track featuring keyboard and guitar solos, “Any Colour You Like” has no lyrics or sound clips but many can interpret the song’s message to be – you are the master of your universe, your own destiny so make it any colour you like. Ironically, the actual meaning of the title is the complete opposite – it refers to the illusion of choice.

This is the only Pink Floyd track with songwriting credits to the trio of David Gilmour, Nick Mason, Richard Wright before Wright’s death in 2008. (2014’s Endless River album featured many posthumous tracks.)

It was composed and recorded while Roger Waters was away on the holidays. Therefore, the bass track is one that was recorded for “Breathe”. In the album’s tablature book it is even listed as “Breathe (Second Reprise)” due to the fact that the chord sequence is nearly the same, but transposed down from E minor to D minor.
