## About
Almost 6 minutes long, and yet, it feels as if this is a whole group together, not just a solo project. Whatever the reason, “Cluster One” is the perfect fit for the opener. Starting off with a clip of an electromagnetic solar pulse, causing some hilariously to think their copy of The Division Bell is broken, the song then breaks off into waves of synths and then, an orchestrated cause led by a grand piano, full of reversed piano samples and synth pads, as well as an orchestra. While so, “Cluster One” helps the magnum opus grow gradually.
