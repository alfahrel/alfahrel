## About
“Welcome to the Machine” is the 2nd track on Pink Floyd’s 9th studio album, Wish You Were Here.

The song could be interpreted in two main ways:

- Pink Floyd’s own scathing view of the music industry;
- a dystopic vision of the present or the future society in which we live; this society is depicted as a machine that shapes our lives since our birth (in a Huxley’s “Brave New World” fashion).
