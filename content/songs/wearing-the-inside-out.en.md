## About
The final lead vocal from Richard Wright on a Pink Floyd album, “Wearing The Inside Out” is also the only Pink Floyd track to feature no writing credit to either David Gilmour or Roger Waters since The Dark Side Of The Moon, 21 years prior to this album.

The music was composed by Richard Wright, while the lyrics were provided by Anthony Moore.

Inside Out would eventually become the title of Nick Mason’s retrospective book about the history of Pink Floyd.
