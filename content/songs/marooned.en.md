## About
“Marooned” is the fourth track on Pink Floyd’s 1994 studio album The Division Bell.

It earned a Grammy Award in 1995 in the category “Best Rock Instrumental Performance”. It was the only Grammy that Pink Floyd ever received.

It was chosen to appear on the 2001 compilation album Echoes: The Best of Pink Floyd.

For the 20th anniversary of the release of The Division Bell in 2014, a music video was produced by Aubrey Powell of Hipgnosis.

In honor of their previous accomplishments and of tribute to the late Richard Wright, Pink Floyd’s The Endless River was released. “Night Light”, Track 10 off it, reminisces off this track.
